## Summary

This change follows up #3263 and #3302. It moves the drift tests listed in
#3309 off whole-file flattening for **presence** pins. Each pin is now narrowed
with `section()`/`readRepoDoc()`/`flat()` from
`worker/deno/tests/support/markdown_docs.ts` to the heading that carries its
rule. Absence checks stay whole-file.

- **Converted in place:** `coding_guidelines_additive_member_3137`,
  `coding_guidelines_meaning_change_2904`, `coding_guidelines_twin_drift`,
  `documentation_audit_prompt_v9`, `hidden_allowlist_drift`,
  `issue_prompt_workflow_files`, `reserved_label_warning_v2826`,
  `security_scan_defensive_labels` and `test_category_definitions`.
- **Split into a `*_drift_test.ts` file:** `prompt_presence_gaps`,
  `severity_emoji_scale` and `suppression_governance_drift` now have
  `prompt_presence_gaps_drift_test.ts`, `severity_emoji_scale_drift_test.ts`
  and `suppression_governance_drift_drift_test.ts`. Their section pins moved
  there because `markdown_docs.ts` spawns git (the #3302 `HEAVY_RE` hazard).
  None of the 18 touched files is named in `lib/completeness_checks.ts` or
  `tests/completeness_checks_test.ts`.
- **Left whole-file, with a header comment giving the reason:**
  `overflow_tracker_scope` and `scan_prompt_open_issue_titles` (loops over
  every prompt directory), and the "every surface counting the catalogue says
  thirteen" pin in `test_audit_unit_suite_checks_943`. That pin's three claims
  sit in three different sections.
- `test_audit_unit_suite_checks_943`'s `checkBody` now passes the check number
  through `escapeRegExp` before building its heading regex.
- Not touched by this diff: `idle_task_live_recheck_dedup_3045`,
  `github_actions_audit_cost_group`, `merge_conflict_prompt_v2`,
  `pr_body_matches_final_diff_3015`, `pr_claims_verified_3058`,
  `prompt_docs_sweep_3172`, `prompt_docs_sweep_2952`, `prompt_docs_sweep_3073`,
  `security_tree_sweep`, `timing_assertion_policy` and
  `unfenced_untrusted_text`.
